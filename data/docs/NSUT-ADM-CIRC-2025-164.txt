Circular
B.Tech./B.Arch. First Semester Students – Academic Year 2025 – 26
The provisional schedule of academic activities for newly admitted candidates is outlined below.
1. Orientation and Induction Programme: Details of Orientation and Student Induction Programme will be notified separately (tentative dates: 30.07.2025 - 31.07.2025).
2. Fee payment schedule: The balance fee submission portal for provisionally admitted candidates at NSUT will open on 27th July 2025 from 5:00 PM onwards.
Details and procedure for balance fee submission will be emailed to the provisionally admitted candidates on 27th July 2025. The balance fee is Rs. 1,55,000/- for B.Tech. and Rs. 1,67,000/- for B.Arch. Kindly refer to the notice displayed on https://www.imsnsit.org/imsnsit/notifications.php .
3. Date of approval of course Registration: Students are required to report to the campus in person for course registration approval on 1st August 2025. Kindly refer to the notice displayed on https://www.imsnsit.org/imsnsit/notifications.php .
4. Commencement of classes: Classes will commence from 04.08.2025 (Monday).
5. Hostel Accommodation: A notification regarding hostel accommodation has been issued and is available at https://www.imsnsit.org/imsnsit/notifications.php .
6. Anti ragging Undertaking: Students are advised to visit the following anti-ragging website, follow the provided instructions carefully, and complete the required details: https://antiragging.in/affidavit_university_form.php .
A candidate's registration will be approved only after the balance fee payment and physical reporting on campus. For any admission-related issues, students may contact the Admission Cell (Room No. 16, Admin Block).
Students are advised to regularly visit http://www.nsut.ac.in (Notices/Circulars Tab) for all updates. Additionally, notices and circulars can be viewed at https://www.imsnsit.org/imsnsit/notifications.php .
