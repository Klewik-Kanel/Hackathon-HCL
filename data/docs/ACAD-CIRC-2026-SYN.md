# Office of the Dean (Academics): Circular on Minimum Attendance

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT written by the hackathon team to test supersession. Not issued by NSUT.

Circular No. ACAD-CIRC-2026-SYN. Date of issue: 20 September 2026. Effective from: 1 January 2027.

## 1. Minimum attendance for examinations

1.1 With effect from 1 January 2027, a student must have a minimum attendance of 80% of the total classes held in a subject, including lectures, tutorials and practicals, to be eligible to appear in the mid-semester and end-semester examinations of that subject.

1.2 This clause supersedes clause 11.2 of the Regulations for B.Tech Programmes 2019-I(A) for all B.Tech students.

1.3 The relaxation provisions in clauses 11.3 to 11.6 of the Regulations remain unchanged.
